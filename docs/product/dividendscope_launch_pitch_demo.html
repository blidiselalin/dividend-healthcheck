The job to be done

Investors do not need more numbers. They need answers they can trust.

DividendScope is designed for the investor who has outgrown a spreadsheet but does not want a trading terminal.

01 · Past

What did I actually receive?

Broker exports, taxes, duplicate imports, currencies, and payment dates can make a simple total surprisingly hard to verify.

Answer: reconciled gross, tax, and net cash.
02 · Present

Which income is becoming fragile?

Yield alone does not explain payout coverage, growth durability, debt sensitivity, or concentration inside the portfolio.

Answer: prioritized, explainable risk signals.
03 · Future

What should I expect next?

Received, accrued, declared, and estimated income are often blended together, weakening planning and confidence.

Answer: clearly labelled forward income.

How the demo treats dividend income

These definitions are intentionally visible because different certainty levels should not be blended into one unexplained total.

Gross receivedA completed broker cash transaction before withholding tax and other deductions.
Withholding taxTax deducted by the broker or payment source and shown separately from the dividend itself.
Net receivedGross received minus withholding tax and any applicable deductions.
Estimated next 12 monthsA forward-looking projection based on sample holdings and payment assumptions. It is not guaranteed cash.
