# DividendScope v0.1.0-beta.1

**Status:** Draft prerelease notes — do not publish until owner placeholders are resolved and PR 2–5 gates pass.  
**Channel:** Public beta / prerelease  
**Version:** `0.1.0-beta.1`

## Release summary

DividendScope v0.1.0-beta.1 is the first controlled external release of an educational dividend analytics application. It offers a public sample-data demo and invite-only access to real portfolios for approved beta users.

This is a **prerelease**. Features may change. Availability and data completeness are not guaranteed.

## Who the beta is for

- Visitors exploring the product with sample data
- A small cohort of approved dividend investors evaluating real-portfolio workflows
- Contributors who want clear security, privacy, and contribution entry points

It is **not** for unrestricted public registration, trading, or production-critical financial operations.

## Main capabilities

- Public sample portfolio (educational demo)
- Google OAuth sign-in
- Invite-only real portfolio workspace
- Manual holdings and authenticated broker-file import (existing supported formats)
- Dividend income views with distinction between received and estimated figures where the app already separates them
- Portfolio health / attention indicators
- Research and scoring views presented as informational signals
- Documented support, privacy, and security contacts

See [BETA_SCOPE.md](BETA_SCOPE.md) for the inclusion/exclusion list.

## Known limitations

- Pre-release quality; no uptime or market-data accuracy guarantee
- Market and dividend data may be delayed or incomplete
- Scores and labels are educational research categories, not personalized advice
- No automatic IBKR sync, trading, payments, or public real-portfolio sharing
- Licensed under MIT; copyright holder placeholder in `LICENSE` must be replaced before publish
- Self-service export/deletion may remain a documented manual process for this beta
